Features

Everything you need
for precise measurement work

A powerful backend paired with a clean interface — designed to save time and reduce errors.

Persistent Database

Every operation you run is automatically saved to your personal database. Access your full history anytime, filter it, or export it as CSV.

Smart Unit Conversion

Convert between length, weight, volume, and temperature units instantly. The system validates compatibility and shows you exactly what happens.

Secure Authentication

JWT-based authentication keeps your data private. Register once and your entire history is tied to your account — accessible from anywhere.

Usage Analytics

Track how many operations of each type you've run. Live counters update in real-time so you always see the full picture of your usage.

Error Logging

Failed operations are captured and stored separately in an error log. Diagnose issues easily with full context on what went wrong and when.

Dark & Light Modes

Switch between a sleek dark interface and a clean light theme. Your preference is remembered across sessions on every page.
